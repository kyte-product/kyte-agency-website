A practical guide to better product briefs

Sample resource for template review.

Clarify the problem
Describe the situation, who experiences it and why it matters. Separate the evidence you have from the assumptions you still need to check.

Define success
Write down the decision this brief should support, the intended outcome and the constraints the team needs to work within.

Use the brief together
Review the brief with design, product and development. Assign open questions and keep the brief current as you learn.
