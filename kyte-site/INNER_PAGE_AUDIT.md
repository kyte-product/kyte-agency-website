# Kyte inner-page design audit

9 October 2026. Scope: every active route except the homepage and its archived snapshot. Compared against the refined homepage, the working design system, and the supplied Goodface, Pineapple, ANAND, and Stripe references. Reference layouts informed hierarchy and card composition; Kyte content and tokens remain in use.

| Route or pattern | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `/work` | Cards used a separate uppercase metadata row, large standalone client title, and smaller body copy. | Matched the homepage image-first project cards: 16:9 media, 8px corners, 28px grid gap, 20px summary, subdued category below. |
| `/work/[slug]` and `/work/collectbee` | Back links, headings, metadata, and section rules differed. Short rules above each fact made the page look fragmented. | Aligned back actions with the page inset, unified the title and summary scale, and moved case-study facts beside the title without a top rule. Inset the cover and made lone gallery images full width, with no more than two images per row. Removed routine section dividers from the Collectbee template. |
| `/insights` | Imported topic cards used unexplained blue circles and side rules. The slanted transition and rows added visual noise. | Removed the topic-card row and slanted transition. Kept the featured story, filters, and article browsing. Story cards now use the same media ratio and spacing system as Work. |
| `/insights/[slug]` | Sticky metadata rail introduced another divider pattern and narrowed the reading area. | Moved author and date into one full-width metadata row, aligned the back link with case studies, and set a consistent reading width. |
| Website Design & Development service | The page had its own heading and button sizes, pill corners, circular FAQ and priority symbols, short decorative rules, and an oversized project-row treatment. | Applied the shared heading and button tokens, 8px controls, Lucide plus/minus FAQ symbols, and the homepage project-card structure for selected work. Removed the priority orbit and benefit-card top rules. |
| Remaining IA routes | These routes are still intentional preview placeholders, not finished service, industry, legal, or company pages. Their narrow centered layout was inconsistent with the live pages. | Aligned placeholder inset, back link, heading, and body type with inner-page patterns. Full page content remains to be designed and approved route by route. |
| Design system | Inner-page cards, divider discipline, and back links were undocumented; two color notes still described violet controls. | Documented those patterns and corrected the controls to cobalt. |

Shared header and footer were inspected as common site elements and left visually unchanged. The homepage was not edited. Work and Design News still use unpublished Sanity drafts only in local development; production lists remain empty until editorial entries are approved and published. Collectbee imagery and impact figures in the local template remain explicit placeholders. Legal and most IA pages remain incomplete previews and must not be treated as launch-ready content.

Checks: `npm run lint`, `npm run build`, and local browser review of Work, both Collectbee routes, Design News index, a Design News article, and the Website Design & Development service page. Design News filtering was checked with one topic and reset to All. The production build completed; Sanity CDN DNS was unavailable from the build sandbox, so live published-content rendering was not verified. Desktop browser screenshots were reviewed. A narrow viewport visual review remains before release.
